# MrShinyWhiteTiger
## Full title: *The Incredible Mr. Shiny in: The White Tiger Hunt*

Take on the role of the world's sharpest bounty hunter, **Mr. Shiny**, as he races against the clock to scale the White Tiger smuggling ring's **fortified tower** and take down the **sinister Director Baek**. Use his signature **grappling hook** to zip around the tower and outmaneuver your enemies, and earn **style points** for taking them out in the flashiest way possible! Can you take out the White Tiger **before time runs out?**

---

*The Incredible Mr. Shiny in: The White Tiger Hunt* is an arcade-style brawler game by Alec Pike. 

---

## Instructions

### Controls

This game is intended to be played with a game controller (this guide assumes a PlayStation controller), and will not work very well with a keyboard. 

* Left analog stick: move
* Right analog stick: camera
* ✕ (bottom face button): jump
* ○ (right face button): dodge
* □ (left face button): light attack
* △ (top face button): heavy attack
* Right trigger: grappling hook
* Left trigger: (with full Shiny Meter) activate Shiny Mode

### Winning and Losing

Climb up to the top of the tower and defeat Director Baek to complete the mission! If the clock runs out or the White Tiger goons beat you, you fail. 

### Style and Scoring

Whenever you do something flashy or stylish, you earn *Style Points.* Defeating enemies, dodging attacks perfectly, and using varied attacks all earn you Style Points. Sure, you could just blindly button-mash your way through, but it's a lot more stylish to spice things up!

As you earn Style Points, you will fill your *Shiny Meter.* When the meter is full (indicated by a star appearing at the end of it), you can press the left trigger to enter *Shiny Mode,* doubling your speed, strength, jump height, and Style Points earned until the meter runs out. Successfully landing attacks while in Shiny Mode also restores your health!

If you successfully complete the mission, you will be shown your final score for the mission, which is your total collected Style Points plus bonus points for time left on the clock. How high can you score?
